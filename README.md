# The Little Book of Accessibility — Claude Skill

A Claude skill that transforms how organisations think about, design, and deliver accessibility and inclusive experiences.

This skill acts as a **strategic advisor, systems thinker, and organisational transformation mentor** — helping people move beyond compliance checklists towards genuine, measurable inclusion.

> *"Designing for everyone means everyone receives a designed experience."*

---

## What This Skill Does

The Little Book of Accessibility gives Claude deep, structured knowledge across the full spectrum of organisational accessibility: strategy, governance, design, operations, culture, and business value. It brings a mentor's perspective rather than a generic assistant's — it challenges assumptions, connects accessibility to wider organisational goals, and provides operationally realistic guidance grounded in real-world context.

It covers:
- Accessibility as a strategic and commercial capability
- Inclusive design principles and practice
- Governance, policy, and executive accountability
- The European Accessibility Act and POUR+S framework
- Cognitive accessibility and multimodal design
- AI governance and responsible innovation
- Organisational transformation and change management
- The employee lifecycle from hire to exit
- Measurement, maturity, and evidence-based improvement

## Who This Skill Is For

### Executives and Board Members
Understanding how accessibility connects to ESG commitments, brand reputation, legal risk, and long-term commercial performance. Getting clear answers on what executive sponsorship actually requires — and why compliance alone is not enough.

**Key tasks:** Building the business case · Framing accessibility in board reporting · Understanding EAA obligations · Connecting inclusion to ESG and governance strategy · Setting meaningful KPIs

---

### Accessibility Managers and Leads
Day-to-day operational and strategic guidance for people responsible for delivering accessibility across an organisation. Moving from reactive remediation to proactive, embedded capability.

**Key tasks:** Developing accessibility strategy · Managing POUR+S frameworks · Building champions networks · Creating testing maturity programmes · Navigating the EAA · Measuring and reporting outcomes · Handling trade-offs under constraint

---

### Designers and UX Professionals
Practical, principled guidance on inclusive and cognitive design — from foundational principles through to design system decisions, multimodal interaction, and content design.

**Key tasks:** Applying cognitive design principles · Designing for multimodal interaction · Writing meaningful alt text · Managing captions and audio description · Understanding affordances and conventions · Designing accessible components · Balancing brand with accessibility requirements

---

### Product Managers and Owners
Help embedding accessibility into product strategy, prioritisation decisions, and delivery planning — without losing momentum or compromising inclusion.

**Key tasks:** Integrating accessibility into roadmaps · Managing accessibility trade-offs · Framing inclusion as product quality · Applying shift-left accessibility · Setting acceptance criteria · Responding to scope and budget pressure

---

### HR, People, and Talent Leaders
Designing inclusive employee experiences across the full lifecycle — from recruitment and onboarding through to development, progression, and exit.

**Key tasks:** Auditing each lifecycle stage for accessibility barriers · Supporting manager capability · Embedding workplace adjustments · Improving disabled employee retention · Designing accessible onboarding · Aligning HR practices with disability inclusion commitments

---

### Governance, Risk, and Compliance Professionals
Understanding and evidencing accessibility obligations — particularly under the European Accessibility Act — and embedding accessibility into procurement, supplier management, and organisational policy.

**Key tasks:** Understanding EAA functional requirements · Developing accessibility policies and statements · Embedding accessibility in procurement criteria · Evaluating supplier compliance · Managing accessibility as a risk metric · Aligning with legal and regulatory obligations

---

### Communications, Marketing, and Brand Teams
Designing inclusive campaigns, content, and brand experiences — and understanding how accessible communications strengthen brand trust, reach, and loyalty.

**Key tasks:** Inclusive brand strategy · Barrier-free communications · Accessible content design · Caption and audio description for media · Alt text ownership · Avoiding otherness in accessibility language · Understanding the commercial value of inclusive marketing

---

### Change Management and Transformation Professionals
Embedding accessibility into organisational change programmes, culture initiatives, and transformation efforts — ensuring inclusion is systemic rather than episodic.

**Key tasks:** Establishing champions and sponsor frameworks · Building accessibility culture · Designing training pathways · Managing knowledge sharing · Scaling inclusion programmes · Sustaining momentum beyond initial commitments

---

### Developers and Engineers
Understanding the strategic and governance context behind accessibility requirements, and connecting technical implementation decisions to human and organisational outcomes.

**Key tasks:** Understanding WCAG as a foundation not a ceiling · Robust and standards-based development · Accessible design systems and components · Assistive technology compatibility · Progressive enhancement · Accessibility in definition of done

## Installation

This is a Claude skill for use with the [Claude](https://claude.ai) platform.

### Repository Structure

```
little-book-of-accessibility/
├── README.md               ← This file
├── SKILL.md                ← Skill definition, behaviour, and reference routing
└── references/
    ├── 01-core-inclusion-accessibility-thinking.md
    ├── 02-inclusion-governance-culture-policy-bias.md
    ├── 03-operational-inclusion-systems.md
    ├── 04-strategic-business-value-transformation.md
    └── 05-reasoning-mentoring-evaluation.md
```

### How to Install

1. Download or clone this repository.
2. In Claude, open **Settings → Skills**.
3. Upload the `SKILL.md` file and the `references/` folder together.
4. The skill will be available in any conversation where the topic relates to accessibility, inclusive design, or organisational inclusion.

The skill loads reference files on demand — only those relevant to the current question are used, keeping responses focused and efficient.

## Design Principles of This Skill

- **Mentor, not assistant** — challenges assumptions, identifies blind spots, connects dots across the organisation
- **Strategic and operational** — works at board level and delivery level equally
- **Evidence-based** — grounds guidance in real-world context, not idealism
- **Human-centred** — keeps disabled people's experience at the centre, not as an afterthought
- **Anti-theatre** — actively avoids compliance-only framing, accessibility as a tick-box, and performative inclusion
